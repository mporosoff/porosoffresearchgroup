Section A — AutoChem 2920 (T6079)

Sample Prep & Loading

Cleaning the sample tube, quartz wool packing, weighing, and analysis-port installation.

Tag: T6079 Instrument: Micromeritics AutoChem 2920 S/N: 941 Location: G242 Updated: September 2026

01

Dismantle & Clean the Sample Tube

GlovesWear gloves at all times when handling the sample tube; use cotton gloves if the sample chamber is hot.
  1. Dismantle the sample tube carefully: unscrew the large arm first, then the small arm. Make sure the metal retaining nuts do not fall down the tube — they can damage it.
  2. Dispose of any remaining sample in the appropriate waste bin (solid catalyst waste goes in the catalyst waste bin in lab 245).
  3. Clean the sample tube with Alconox (recommended 5 g Alconox per 500 mL) and rinse thoroughly with water. Then clean with acetone or IPA.
  4. Dry the exterior with Kimwipes and dry the tube interior firmly with house air. Keep a proper grip while using house air so the tube does not drop.
02

Pack & Weigh

  1. Place a small amount of quartz wool into the clean, empty sample tube — enough to fill the cone so the thermocouple contacts the sample without pushing against the wool. Use a metal rod to guide the wool into place; do not pack it down.
  2. Weigh the sample tube with quartz wool.
  3. Measure the required amount of sample (about 150 mg) and place it in the tube.
  4. Weigh the sample tube + quartz wool + sample.
03

Install on the Analysis Port

  1. Carefully install the sample tube on the analysis port: slide the retaining nuts and ferrules over the open ends of the tube. The larger nuts and ferrules go on the larger side of the tube; the smaller nuts and ferrules go narrow-end-down on the smaller side.

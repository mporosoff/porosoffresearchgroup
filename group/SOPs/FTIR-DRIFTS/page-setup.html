Section A — FTIR DRIFTS (T6943)

Setup & Sample Prep

Detector cooling, KBr dilution, OMNIC configuration, and loading the Praying Mantis reaction chamber.

01

Before You Start

  1. Check all gas levels (CO₂, H₂, Ar, CO, N₂ — if using for purge).
  2. Open the liquid-nitrogen port and remove the cap. Using a funnel, slowly fill the port with liquid nitrogen until full (liquid nitrogen bubbles out from the funnel base). After the liquid settles, add liquid nitrogen again to ensure the dewar is topped off. Wait at least 1 hour for the MCT detector to cool sufficiently.
  3. Prepare the sample: 50 mg of sample to 500 mg of KBr (1:10 dilution).
  4. Be sure the Brooks Smart Interface software is open. Check that the system is depressurized before removing the cover of the reactor cell — the back-pressure regulator in the Smart Interface should read ~0 psi.
02

Configure OMNIC

  1. Open OMNIC and select the Expt Set icon. Click Open and select the setup file Standard_lab_IR.exp to load the presets.
  2. Collect tab: Number of Scans 99; Resolution 4; Final Format Kubelka-Munk; Correction None (no other boxes checked); Background Handling “Collect background before every sample”. For gain, “auto gain” is often used; the black knob (next to the silver knob that seals the reactor space) is rotated to maximize the peak-to-peak ratio (2–9.8).
  3. Bench tab: Sample Compartment Main; Detector MCT/A; Beamsplitter KBr; Source IR; Accessory DRIFTS; Window None; Range 4000–650 cm⁻¹; Gain Autogain; Optical Velocity 1.8988; Aperture ~150 (as small as possible while maintaining P2P); Sample Shuttle None. Do not change other tabs except Series (configured later).
  4. Save your files in your own folder under C: > My Documents, using a dated folder for each experiment.
03

Load the Sample & Start Support Equipment

  1. Load the sample cup with catalyst at the appropriate KBr dilution (1:10 sample:KBr).
  2. After loading the catalyst, tighten the dome in a cross-wise fashion. Check that all valves in the gas line from the controllers to the back-pressure regulator are open.
  3. Turn on the chiller (I/O switch to I). Press the power button and press ▶ to start running — you should hear the pump turn on.
  4. Turn on the Harrick temperature controller Power and Heater. Instructions are on pages 99–109 of the manual pm pid 1.pdf.

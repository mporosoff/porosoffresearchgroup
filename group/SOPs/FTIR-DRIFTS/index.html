What This Guide Covers

This SOP covers in situ DRIFTS experiments on the Thermo Fisher FTIR spectrometer fitted with the Harrick Praying Mantis diffuse-reflection accessory and high-temperature reaction chamber. Gas delivery is controlled through the Brooks Smart Interface and Brooks mass flow controllers; spectra are collected in OMNIC with the MCT/A detector.

Section A

Setup & Sample Prep

Gas checks, liquid-nitrogen cooling of the MCT detector, KBr dilution, OMNIC experiment configuration, loading the sample cup, chiller and temperature controller startup.

 Section B

Pretreatment & Reaction

Leak checks, background scans, reduction/pretreatment, setting reaction flows, and synchronizing the series collection with the temperature program.

 Section C

Data Analysis

Opening the series file, splitting it into individual spectra, and exporting to .csv for plotting.
Cryogen safetyLiquid nitrogen is used to cool the MCT detector. Wear cryogenic gloves, a lab coat, and eye protection when filling the dewar, and fill slowly through the funnel to avoid splashing.

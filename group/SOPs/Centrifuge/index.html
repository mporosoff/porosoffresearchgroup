Lab Standard Operating Procedure

Centrifuge

Tube loading, balancing, running, decanting, and care. Lab default: 13,000 RPM for 10 minutes.

01

Set Up & Load

  1. Set up the centrifuge conditions: rotations per minute (RPM) and time. The lab default is 13,000 RPM and 10 minutes. This may vary depending on your sample, so check requirements beforehand.
  2. Wash and dry an even number of centrifuge tubes (min 2, max 6), based on your sample amount. Also make sure the blue tube holders inside the machine are clean.
  3. Transfer relatively equal amounts of your samples into the tubes and add DI or MilliQ water so each tube is loaded above 1/2 but less than 2/3 full. Tightly shut the tubes with the accompanying caps.
  4. Make sure all tubes have comparable mass (less than 0.1 g difference).
  5. Press OPEN and gently open the top case until it stands. Inside, press the rotor auto-lock button and turn the seal counterclockwise to open; place the seal to the side.
  6. If further mixing is needed, gently place the tubes on the vortex mixer in “touch” mode one by one for a few seconds. Then load them into the centrifuge symmetrically (e.g., positions 1–4, 2–5, 3–6) to keep the mass balanced.
RPM vs RCFThe maximum RPM for this machine is 15,000 RPM. Relative Centrifugal Field: RCF = 11.18 × r × (RPM/1000)², where r = radius in cm. For the default 13,000 RPM this corresponds to about 24,700 × g.
02

Run & Unload

  1. Place the seal back on and turn clockwise (press the auto-lock button afterwards). Make sure the seal is firmly in place. Close the top lid and press START.
  2. Once the run completes, the machine starts beeping — remove the samples as soon as possible. Failure to do so can result in your sample remixing with the separated solution. Press OPEN and remove the seal as before.
  3. Carefully decant the solution from the tubes into a waste beaker. Dispose of the liquid in the proper waste container depending on its pH. Do not pour it down the sink.
  4. Perform further washing if required by adding more deionized water and repeating the centrifugation.
03

Maintenance & Care

  • Make sure mass is symmetrically distributed before starting. Loud noises during use are a sign the mass is not loaded symmetrically — weigh tubes beforehand to confirm comparable mass.
  • Fill tubes to the correct level (between 1/2 and 2/3) or they might deform.
  • If tubes break or crack inside the machine, carefully take them out and limit spilling. Dry the inside with paper towels, then re-clean using paper towels wetted alternately with water and acetone.
  • Do not misplace the seal — keep it near the machine.
